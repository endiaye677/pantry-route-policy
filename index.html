Pantry Route — Privacy Policy

Effective September 30, 2026 · Updated October 31, 2026

Pantry Route is a delivery-operations app used by a food pantry in the Bridgeview, Illinois area. It is used by pantry coordinators and volunteer delivery drivers — not by the people receiving food. This policy explains what information the app handles and how it is protected.

What we collect, and why

  • Volunteer accounts (coordinators and drivers): name, email address, phone number, an optional device token so anyone assigned a run can receive "you've been assigned a run" notifications, and the version of the app they last signed in with (so coordinators can see who still needs to update). Collected so the pantry can organize deliveries.
  • Team messages and announcements: announcements that coordinators post to the team, and messages between a driver and the pantry coordinators (drivers can’t message each other). Also reports of inappropriate messages, the people a user has blocked, and optional availability (days a volunteer can drive) and reminder settings. Messages are visible only to that driver and the pantry coordinators. Notifications say who wrote, never what was written. Coordinators review reported messages and can hide them.
  • Reports for funders: coordinators can produce a monthly summary with totals only (deliveries, households served, and counts by language and ZIP code). It contains no names, addresses, or phone numbers, and any group of fewer than 5 households is hidden so no family can be singled out.
  • Delivery photos (optional): if the pantry turns this on, a driver may take a photo after a delivery (for example, of a package left at the door). Location and all other photo details are removed before the photo is saved. Only the driver who took it and the pantry coordinators can see it — never other drivers — and only through a link that expires within minutes. Photos are deleted automatically after 30 days. The app uses the camera only when the driver chooses to take a delivery photo and never reads the photo library.
  • Household delivery information, entered by pantry staff on behalf of the families served: a contact name, phone number, street address and map location, delivery notes (e.g., "use the side door"), preferred text-message language, and a record of deliveries made. Collected solely to deliver food and honor the pantry's monthly delivery limits.
What we don't do. No advertising, no analytics or tracking SDKs, no sale or rental of any information, ever. Driver locations are not tracked: the pantry's servers never receive or store a driver's location (see “Location during navigation”).

Location during navigation

Drivers can get turn-by-turn directions inside the app. Only then does the app use the phone's location:

  • On a delivery run, only while in use. The app asks for location when a driver taps Start on a run, never at launch, and only “while using the app” — never “always”. It then uses the location for three things on that run: to plan the route from where the driver is; to guide them turn by turn from stop to stop; and to keep guiding them to their finish point (the pantry or Home) after the last stop is done. One use is outside a run: in Account → Home, “Use my current location” reads the phone's position once, when the driver taps it, to save Home from where they are (the same “while using the app” permission; nothing is read until they tap). Guidance keeps running with the screen locked (iPhone shows the blue location indicator; Android shows an ongoing “navigating” notification), and location use stops when guidance ends, when the driver reaches their finish, or when they end it.
  • Never on our servers. The driver's position is used on the phone to guide them. It is not sent to the pantry's servers, not stored there, and not shown to coordinators or other drivers.
  • Mapbox. To draw and update the route, the app sends Mapbox coordinates: the driver's current position and the stops (and, if the driver chose one, where the route should end). Requests carry no names, addresses, or household details. Mapbox telemetry is turned off: no location or trip data goes to Mapbox for its own use. Mapbox's software does send a short count when maps or navigation start, which Mapbox uses to bill the pantry: the phone model, system and map-software versions, whether location is allowed, and an ID for this app on the phone. It contains no location.
  • Home stays on the phone. A driver may save a Home so the route can end there, either from their current position or by typing an address (which is sent to Mapbox to look it up). After that, Home is kept only on the driver's phone, used with Mapbox only as coordinates, never sent to the pantry's servers, and erased when they sign out.
  • Optional. A driver who declines location can still deliver: the app opens their usual maps app instead.

Who processes this information

  • Supabase hosts the database and the private storage for delivery photos (United States), with row-level security restricting every user to the minimum they need. Drivers see a household’s address and phone number only while it is on their own active delivery run. Before a driver takes on a delivery, they see only the household’s first name and last initial and its neighborhood (or city and ZIP code); when two households would look the same, a street name without the house number, or a plain number, tells them apart. On the map, drivers can see the remaining stops of other drivers’ runs for the same day only as approximate points — the center of an area about 200 m across, with no names or addresses. Such a point can happen to fall near or on a home, and because points disappear as stops are completed, someone watching the map over time could tell roughly when a stop in that area was served.
  • Twilio sends the delivery text messages (phone number and message content only) and connects drivers’ calls to families through the pantry’s number, so neither side sees the other’s personal number (Twilio receives both numbers to connect the call; calls are not recorded). Replies of STOP, START and HELP are used only to update or answer the household’s text preference; the text of other replies is not stored.
  • Mapbox provides maps, directions and address search. The app requests map tiles; route, navigation and route-ordering requests contain only coordinates (the stops, the driver's current position while navigating, and an optional end point such as the driver's Home), never names, addresses, or household details. Addresses are sent to Mapbox as text only to look them up: when pantry staff type a family's address (through the pantry's server, which also limits how often it can be called), and when a driver types their own Home or another place for their route to end (directly from the phone; it is never sent to the pantry's servers). Mapbox telemetry is turned off. Mapbox's software sends Mapbox a short count when maps or navigation start, used to bill the pantry: the phone model, system and map-software versions, whether location is allowed, and an ID for this app on the phone (not linked to the person's name or account). It contains no location.
  • Apple / Google / Expo deliver driver push notifications (a device token and the notification text only).

Each processor receives only what its job requires. None of them may use this information for their own purposes.

SMS Consent

Delivery text notifications are operated by PorSignal LLC on behalf of the food pantry program. Recipients opt in when they register for home delivery, on the pantry’s registration form or verbally. For verbal consent, staff use this exact script:

“Would you like to receive text messages about your food deliveries — a heads-up on delivery day and a message when the driver is on the way? You can reply STOP at any time to opt out.”

Texts from a volunteer driver. Until the pantry’s own texting line is active, a volunteer driver may text a household from the driver’s own phone to say they are on the way. The app opens the phone’s messaging app with a suggested message; the driver reads it and sends it. Such a text comes from the driver’s own number, is never sent automatically, and does not use location. The app records only that a text was started (which household, which delivery, when), never its content. Households that asked for no texts, or that replied STOP, are not texted.

Staff record the household’s answer, with the date and the staff member who recorded it. Only households that say yes receive texts. Message frequency depends on your deliveries (typically 1–2 messages per delivery day); message and data rates may apply.

Mobile numbers and SMS consent are never shared with or sold to third parties for marketing.

  • STOP: reply STOP to any delivery text to opt out immediately — you'll receive no further texts. You can also tell your pantry coordinator at any time.
  • HELP: reply HELP to any delivery text for assistance information, or contact your pantry coordinator.

Retention & deletion

Delivery records are kept for the pantry's operational and grant reporting needs. A household may ask the pantry at any time to correct its information or remove it entirely; a volunteer's account is deactivated (and may be deleted on request) when they stop volunteering. Deleting your account in the app also deletes the messages you sent, your message thread, reports you filed, your blocks, and your availability and reminder settings. Announcements you posted as a coordinator stay, without your name. Delivery photos are deleted automatically after 30 days, and within an hour when the delivery or the household’s record is removed (they can no longer be viewed from the moment of removal).

Delete your account

This section is for the people who sign in to the Pantry Route app: pantry coordinators and volunteer delivery drivers. You can delete your account yourself at any time, with or without the app.

In the app: open Pantry Route and sign in → tap Account on your home screen → scroll to Danger zone → tap Delete account → confirm Delete account. The deletion happens at once and signs you out of every device.

Without the app: email hadji@portsignal.org from the address you signed up with, subject “Delete my Pantry Route account”. We confirm by email and complete the deletion within 30 days, usually within a few days.

What is deleted: your name, email address, phone number, password, sign-in sessions and push-notification token; the messages you sent and your message thread; reports you filed; your blocks; your availability and reminder settings. Anything kept only on your phone, such as a saved Home finish point, goes when you delete the app.

What is kept, and for how long: if you drove deliveries or registered households, the pantry’s delivery records (which run, which stop, when, and whether it was delivered) and the log of account role changes are kept for the pantry’s operational and grant reporting, for as long as that reporting requires. From the moment you delete your account they show “Deleted account” instead of your name and carry no email or phone number. Announcements you posted as a coordinator stay, without your name. Delivery photos are deleted automatically 30 days after the delivery regardless.

Families who receive deliveries are not app users and have no account; see “Your choices” below for correcting or removing a household’s details.

Your choices

If your family receives deliveries and you want your details corrected or removed, or you no longer wish to receive delivery text messages, tell your pantry coordinator — or reply STOP to any delivery text.

Contact
